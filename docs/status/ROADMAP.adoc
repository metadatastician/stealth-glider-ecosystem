= Stealth Glider Line Roadmap

This is the series roadmap. Each member keeps its own roadmap for its own
scope; this document records the order of the line and what is known about
each stage.

== The seven stages

----
f19-stealth-glider -> f117a-stealth-glider -> sr71-blackglider -> x15-rocket-glider -> planer-1 -> enterglide-ncc-137-k -> tardis-rule-disruption
----

. *f19-stealth-glider* (Operation Nightstep v2), shipped. Established substrate
  interaction, phase and causal TRACE.
. *f117a-stealth-glider* (Operation Nightglass), shipped. Added radar and a
  proven-inert 3D camera over the 2D world.
. *sr71-blackglider* (SR-71 BlackGlider), in progress. A single uncompromised
  Conway spacetime with proof-bearing geometric flight presentation.
. *x15-rocket-glider*, reserved, intent recorded. Resource mechanics:
  munitions and fuel on the Life field. The full name is X15-rocket-glider;
  it is not to be shortened to X-15.
. *planer-1* (Планер-1), reserved, intent recorded. Orbits around Riemann
  spheres. Планер is Russian for glider or sailplane; the name is a
  Спутник-1 joke, and the slug is a transliteration, not "planar".
. *enterglide-ncc-137-k* (Enterglide NCC-137-K), reserved, intent recorded.
  Topology of knots (the K). NCC nods to astronomy's catalogue acronyms
  (and the Star Trek registry); 137 points at the Varying-Constants
  hypothesis. Spaces become dashes in the slug.
. *tardis-rule-disruption* (TARDIS), reserved, intent recorded.
  Totally-Adherent Rule-Disruption Imitation System: make a Conway Game of
  Life set-up look as much like reality as possible while never breaking
  B3/S23. See the stage intents below.

== Stage intents (recorded 2026-09-02, owner ruling)

These are the owner's stated intents for the four reserved stages. Intent is
not work begun: each stage keeps CLADE phase `reserved` until its first
content lands by pull request in its own repository. Until 2026-09-02 these
intents existed only in conversation; this section is their durable home.

=== Stage 4, X15-rocket-glider

Resource-type things arrive here: munitions and fuel, spent and replenished
on the live Conway field, so that every move has a cost as well as a lawful
successor state. How the resources are represented on the field, and what
gates they must pass, is not yet specified.

=== Stage 5, Планер-1

Handles orbits around Riemann spheres, taking the line from a flat field to
motion around a compactified plane. The name is a joke on Спутник-1: Планер
is the Russian word for a glider or sailplane (a powered plane would be
самолёт), so Планер-1 reads as "Glider-1", the orbital one. The slug
`planer-1` is the transliteration; it is not "planar", which is precisely
what a Riemann sphere is not.

=== Stage 6, Enterglide NCC-137-K

Starts handling the topology of knots; that is what the K at the end of the
code refers to. NCC is a reference to the galaxy and space catalogue acronyms
of astronomy, with the Star Trek registry echo intended. 137 points at the
"Varying Constants" hypothesis: the idea that the equivalence around the
number 137, the inverse fine-structure constant, may change over time. That
is the focus.

=== Stage 7, TARDIS

The full thing, a super step ahead of the rest of the line: find a way to
make a Conway Game of Life set-up look as much like "reality" as it can
possibly be, with the absolute requirement of never breaking the laws. The
acronym leads with *Totally* on purpose (Totally-Adherent Rule-Disruption
Imitation System): it is about *appearing* to break the rules even though
that is not what happens.

Two parts are recorded separately:

* Hard invariant: B3/S23 is never broken. This is checkable.
* Open target: the result resembles reality. This stays unmeasured until a
  measure is chosen, and is deliberately not a gate yet.

It is a lifetime, open-ended research project, probably never to be
completed and quite possibly impossible. The hope is that after all the
mathematics games and demonstrations along the way, people will think that
maybe anything is possible, or close to it, and take it off in other
directions with weird applications. Even if the reality target is out of
reach, the work is adaptable as a maths challenge, an educational piece on
constraints, or game-design thinking. The owner's own tagline, offered as a
joke: "maybe I'm the next Euclid... Euclid with cells rather than the
floorboard tiles."
